## Description
```
Reclip is the fast way to turn a plain screen recording into a video that looks professionally produced — without a video editor.

RECORD
• Capture a full display or a single window
• Add system audio, your microphone, and your webcam
• Cursor movement is tracked so Reclip can zoom for you

POLISH (no skills needed)
• Automatic zoom that follows your cursor to the action
• Styled backgrounds — gradients and solids
• Padding, rounded corners, and a soft drop shadow
• Webcam bubble overlay you can place in any corner
• Text captions on a timeline
• Trim the start and end; speed up or slow down

EXPORT
• High-quality MP4
• Looping animated GIF
• Live preview of every change before you export

Reclip runs entirely on your Mac. No account, no upload, no watermark.
```
